Example Engagement Paths

Scope note: These are examples of services SSIK is prepared to discuss, not published client case studies. The final scope, methods, deliverables, and timeline are agreed upon after an initial consultation.

Example Scope
01
Security Baseline Review

Review access practices, exposed services, endpoint safeguards, and core security controls, then organize findings into practical priorities.

Example Scope
02
Cyber Risk Prioritization

Identify technology risks, document their business impact and likelihood, and create a clear mitigation roadmap for decision-makers.

Example Scope
03
Policy Foundations

Develop or refine practical policies for acceptable use, access control, account management, data handling, and incident reporting.

Example Scope
04
Infrastructure Health Review

Map critical systems, networking, backups, identity services, and operational dependencies to reveal reliability and planning gaps.

Example Scope
05
Security Improvement Planning

Convert identified gaps into practical actions, owners, dependencies, and phases without presenting the work as a formal certification audit.

Example Scope
06
IT Improvement Roadmap

Turn discovery findings into a phased plan that balances security, reliability, budget, staffing, and operational priorities.
